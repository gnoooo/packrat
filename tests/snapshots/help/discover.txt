usage: packrat discover [-h] <sections|taste|queues|albums|radio> ...

Find new music to download. Pick one of the choices below.

positional arguments:
  <sections|taste|queues|albums|radio>
    sections            popular music from Home, Charts, Moods and New
                        Releases
    taste               find music similar to what you already like (plan
                        only)
    queues              playlists based on YouTube Music radio queues
    albums              whole albums from artists you like
    radio               a radio station based on one song

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
